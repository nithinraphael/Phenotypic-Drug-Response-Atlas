# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cancer and drug-response researchers exploring measured viability, cell morphology, and model predictions.

## Product Purpose

Phenotypic Drug-Response Atlas connects descriptive PRISM screening with per-condition dose-response exploration and a separate BBBC021 microscopy workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four public tabs: Discoveries, Drug Explorer, Cell Explorer, Validation. Keep all calculations, data, caches and audited results intact. Development tools remain separate and accessible. Use existing marimo and Plotly, minimal CSS, no custom JavaScript or model training. Target 16 GB RAM and 4 GB GPU memory. Measurements, conditioned model predictions and heuristic response ranges remain distinct. Static curves cannot establish biological tipping points. PRISM and BBBC021 are unpaired assays. Unverified embeddings, external comparisons and uncertainty remain unavailable.

## Evidence on Hand

Audited local data and shared analysis modules; reports/runs/final-2026-10-26; existing tests and provenance records. Existing microscopy images are real assets. No invented findings, uncertainty bands or model labels.

## Brand Commitments

Natural, concise scientific language. Clear hierarchy, readable figures and accessible desktop/mobile layouts. Publication quality describes presentation, not evidence strength.
